Privacy Policy

This app is a personal publishing tool used only by its owner to upload short video clips to the owner's own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts.

It stores the owner's access tokens locally on the owner's computer, used only to publish videos the owner has chosen to post. It does not collect, sell or share data about any other person, and has no other users.

Clips credit and link the original streamer. To request removal of a clip, contact the account owner through the platform where it was posted.
